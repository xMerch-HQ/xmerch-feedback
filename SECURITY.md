# Security at xMerch

**Report a vulnerability:** use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/mworks-proj/xmerch-testing/security/advisories/new). Only the xMerch team sees it. Please don't open a public issue for a security problem.

**In scope:** xmerch.app and www.xmerch.app, store subdomains (*.xmerch.app), vendors' custom domains served by xMerch, admin.xmerch.app, dex.xmerch.app, and xMerch's Xahau Hooks.

**Out of scope:** reports that only come from automated scanners, missing best-practice headers with no real impact, denial of service, social engineering, and third-party services (Stripe, Xaman, Supabase, Vercel).

**Please:** use your own accounts and test stores, don't access or change other people's data or orders, don't move funds that aren't yours, and give us reasonable time to fix before sharing.

**We'll** reply within 5 business days, keep you updated, and credit you when the fix ships if you'd like. We won't take legal action over good-faith research that follows this policy.

We don't run a paid bug bounty at this time.
